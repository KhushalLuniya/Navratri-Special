नवरात्रि · Navratri

THE DIVINE FESTIVAL OF NINE NIGHTS

❖

Navratri, meaning “nine nights”, is one of India’s most cherished festivals, dedicated to the worship of Goddess Durga and her nine forms, the Navadurga. Across the country, homes and temples glow with lamps, prayers, music and dance.

At its heart is the victory of good over evil, the belief that courage, truth and devotion can overcome darkness. It is a time of fasting, reflection and gratitude, of welcoming positivity, and of beginning anew with a steady heart.

Navratri also celebrates the living diversity of Indian culture: Garba and Dandiya in Gujarat, Durga Puja pandals in the East, Golu displays in the South, Ramlila in the North. Many traditions, one shared devotion.
